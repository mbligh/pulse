Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Pulse (com.mbligh.pulse) is an Android prototype for monitoring evidence of recent phone activity and preparing a manual help request. It is not a medical device or an automatic emergency notification service.

Information accessed

App usage: With your permission, Pulse reads Android usage events such as screen unlocks and apps entering the foreground. It records timestamps, event types, and app package names, along with the last scan and last detected activity.

Location: If you deliberately activate the HELP button, Pulse requests your current or last available location. If available, the prepared message includes coordinates, a map link, estimated accuracy, and the age of the location fix. Pulse does not continuously track your location.

Storage and transmission

Usage-event history and scan information are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. The current prototype keeps at most 100 recent qualifying events. Pulse does not operate its own server or upload this activity history to the developer.

When you activate HELP, Android's share chooser opens with a prepared message. Nothing is sent unless you select a destination and complete the sending action. If you do, the recipient and the selected messaging or sharing provider receive the message according to that provider's own privacy practices.

Pulse uses Google Play services' location API to obtain a location fix; the relevant Google services may process location-related information under Google's own privacy terms. Pulse does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Retention and control

Only the most recent 100 qualifying activity events are retained in the app's local history. You can stop future usage access or location access through Android Settings. Uninstalling Pulse removes its locally stored app data, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. Information you intentionally share through another app is controlled by that app and its recipient.

Children and safety

Pulse is intended for adults. It does not provide medical diagnosis, guaranteed emergency response, or automatic distress alerts. Do not rely on it as your sole means of requesting emergency assistance.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Pulse develops. Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the Pulse project issue tracker. Avoid posting sensitive personal information in public issues.
